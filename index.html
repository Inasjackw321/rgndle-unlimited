      How the score works

      Every roll produces nine digits and one cosmic multiplier. The digits are then examined by a panel of factors, each of which pays out according to how unlikely it is.

      Improbability

      Every roll scores this one. It looks at the roll's shape — how many of each digit you got, ignoring order — and computes the exact probability of that shape. The payout is proportional to 1 / √P, so a shape twice as rare pays about 1.41× as much.

      Arrangement factors

      Improbability is blind to order, so the rest of the panel rewards it: identical runs, ascending and descending straights, palindromes, alternating patterns, arithmetic sequences, repeated halves and trailing zeros.

      Number theory & culture

      The nine digits are also read as a single integer — primes and perfect squares pay out — and scanned for a short list of numbers that people care about for entirely unmathematical reasons.

      Ranks are real

      Ranks are not arbitrary thresholds. The scoring engine was run over millions of simulated rolls, and your rank is your position in that distribution. "Top 0.01%" means exactly that.
